★ Pledge

Support

Need help? Email pledge.citizenship.help@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

I bought Premium but it’s locked

Open Settings in the app (the gear on Today) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with, and make sure you’re online.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pledge, and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Premium stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Pledge has no account, so progress is kept only on your phone. Moving to a new iPhone from a backup (iCloud or your computer) may bring it with you. Premium comes back with Restore purchases.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Pledge in your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications, and that reminders are on in the app’s Settings.

The Listen voice sounds robotic

Download a better voice in your iPhone’s Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices (choose an “Enhanced” English voice), then pick it in the app’s Settings → Reading voice.

Is Pledge from the government?

No. Pledge is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Government. Its practice questions are original, written from the booklet Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond.
