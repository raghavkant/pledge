★ Pledge

Pledge: Citizenship Test AU

Pledge is an iPhone app that helps you prepare for the Australian citizenship test, with practice questions, mock tests and lessons based on the booklet Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond.

It works offline, has no account and no ads, and keeps your progress on your phone.
