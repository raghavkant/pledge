## Competitors (from US results, 2026-09-25)

- **Home Affairs practice test:** ranks top for practice searches. We don't try to beat it or look like it.
- **passaustraliancitizenship.com.au:** clear pass rules, part breakdowns, deep blog clusters (fail, pass rate), an app. Weak: tests 5–14 are paid previews, no page dates, heavy upsell.
- **aussiecitizenshiptest.com:** "EXACTLY REAL EXAM" in titles, "103 tests": volume over honesty.
- **citizenshipprep.com.au, migratio.com.au, citizenshiptest.au:** long-tail blog clusters (test-day checklist, fail, ceremony), often quoting third-party statistics.
- **Migration law firms:** authority on process questions.

**Our edge:** Home Affairs quotes with a "checked on" date, plain English, very fast on mobile, an app-quality practice test with no sign-up, openness about the Department's free resources.
