## Notes and follow-ups

1. **"Official" in content we don't write.** Six free questions from the app use the word in the booklet's sense (for example "the official flag"), and the Terms of Use say "For official information about citizenship and the test, see … Home Affairs". Both are acceptable: rule 6 is about never presenting Pledge as official. See "Decisions for Raghav to review".
2. **No `robots.txt` yet.** On `github.io` it would have to sit at the domain root, which isn't ours. It comes on domain day, as planned. Search engines can still use `sitemap.xml` once it is submitted (steps in `docs/overnight-report.md`).
3. **Fees** are indexed every 1 July: re-check Form 1298i then. All other facts: re-check by 25 December 2026 (90 days).
4. **The Home Affairs conferral page still shows the conflicting "AUD285" fee block**, so the site keeps quoting Form 1298i only.
5. **Rollback copies** (old root files on `main`) can be removed after a week of stable Actions deploys (from about 2 October 2026), as planned.

## How this was checked

- `npm run check:full` steps (build, free tier, legal text, types, budgets, links, axe, Lighthouse, export, paid leak, screenshots), locally and in GitHub's CI.
- A quotation audit: every “…” quote on the built pages matched against today's downloaded Home Affairs pages and the booklet text.
- A runtime audit in Playwright on every page at 390 px and 1440 px: third-party requests, storage, console errors, sideways scrolling.
- Scripted play-throughs of the practice test (marking, values-only fail, focus, keyboard, timer and pause) and the values drill.
- `node scripts/check-links.mjs --external` against the live site, and `curl` of every URL in the sitemap.
